About Us

Born from a simple observation

Our clients were managing too many different vendors for too little time. Luxtremous was created to bring those services together under one roof.

Founder

Daphyre Astremon

After several years in real estate, Daphyre noticed that clients often needed far more than a transaction: reliable customer service, someone to interpret at an important appointment, or simply help keeping their books up to date.

Luxtremous Property LLC was built on that idea — offering real estate and the business services that naturally surround it, all under one address.
