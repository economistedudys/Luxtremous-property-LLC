Overview

Real estate, with the business services around it

Luxtremous Property LLC is a multi-service company built around real estate: buying, selling, and renting property, plus the call center, interpretation, sales, and bookkeeping support that individuals and small businesses need around a transaction.

We work with English, French, and Creole-speaking clients across our service area, with every file tracked by a single point of contact from start to finish.
