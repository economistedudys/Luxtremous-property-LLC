Careers

Build your career with Luxtremous Property LLC

We are a growing multi-service company seeking dependable, detail-oriented professionals across real estate, client services, and finance. Below are our current openings.

Full-Time · Commission-Eligible

Licensed Real Estate Agent

Location: Field / Hybrid  ·  Reports to: Principal Broker

Luxtremous Property LLC is seeking a licensed Real Estate Agent to represent buyers and sellers throughout the transaction process, from initial consultation to closing. The ideal candidate is client-focused, organized, and comfortable managing multiple active files at once.

Responsibilities

  • Guide buyers and sellers through property searches, showings, and offers
  • Prepare and review purchase agreements, disclosures, and closing documents
  • Negotiate terms on behalf of clients and coordinate with lenders, inspectors, and title companies
  • Maintain accurate, up-to-date records for every active listing and client file

Qualifications

  • Active real estate license in good standing (required)
  • 1+ years of experience in residential real estate preferred
  • Strong communication skills in English; French or Creole a plus
  • Reliable transportation and flexible availability for showings
Full-Time or Part-Time

Call Center Representative

Location: Remote / In-Office  ·  Reports to: Operations Lead

We are hiring a Call Center Representative to serve as the first point of contact for our clients and their customers. This role requires a professional phone manner, strong listening skills, and the ability to stay organized across multiple accounts.

Responsibilities

  • Answer inbound calls and respond to client inquiries promptly and professionally
  • Schedule and confirm appointments across multiple calendars
  • Log call details and follow-up actions accurately in our system
  • Escalate complex issues to the appropriate team member

Qualifications

  • Previous customer service or call center experience preferred
  • Clear, professional phone and written communication
  • Comfortable using scheduling and CRM software
  • Bilingual (English/French or English/Creole) strongly preferred
Contract / On-Call

Interpreter (English / French / Creole)

Location: In-Person / Remote  ·  Reports to: Client Services

We are building a roster of qualified interpreters to support clients during medical, legal, and administrative appointments. Assignments are scheduled on an as-needed basis, in person or by phone/video.

Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate, real-time interpretation between English and French or Creole
  • Maintain strict confidentiality of all client information
  • Arrive on time and prepared for scheduled appointments
  • Communicate professionally with clients and the professionals they are meeting with

Qualifications

  • Fluency in English and French or Haitian Creole
  • Prior interpretation experience preferred (medical or legal a plus)
  • Professional demeanor and strong attention to detail
  • Reliable availability for scheduled appointments
Full-Time · Commission-Eligible

Sales Associate

Location: Hybrid  ·  Reports to: Sales Manager

We are looking for a motivated Sales Associate to identify new business opportunities and support client relationships across our service lines. This role is well suited to a self-starter who is comfortable with outbound outreach and consultative selling.

Responsibilities

  • Prospect and qualify new client opportunities across service lines
  • Conduct consultative sales conversations to match clients with the right services
  • Maintain a consistent follow-up cadence with prospects and existing clients
  • Track pipeline activity and report on progress toward targets

Qualifications

  • 1+ years of sales or business development experience preferred
  • Confident, professional communication skills
  • Comfortable working toward monthly targets
  • Bilingual candidates encouraged to apply
Full-Time or Part-Time

Bookkeeper

Location: Remote / Hybrid  ·  Reports to: Finance Lead

We are seeking a detail-oriented Bookkeeper to maintain accurate financial records for our clients. This role requires strong organizational skills, discretion with sensitive financial data, and comfort working across multiple client accounts.

Responsibilities

  • Maintain accurate books for multiple client accounts
  • Reconcile bank and credit card statements monthly
  • Prepare monthly and quarterly financial statements
  • Flag discrepancies and communicate clearly with clients about their finances

Qualifications

  • Experience with QuickBooks or similar accounting software
  • Strong attention to detail and numerical accuracy
  • Associate's degree in accounting or related field preferred
  • Ability to manage confidential financial information responsibly
