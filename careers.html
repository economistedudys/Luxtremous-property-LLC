Licensed Real Estate Agent

Req ID#: LP-10231  ·  Location: Field / Hybrid, Central Florida  ·  Reports to: Principal Broker

Job Description

Luxtremous Property LLC is seeking a licensed Real Estate Agent to represent buyers and sellers throughout the transaction process, from initial consultation to closing. Join a small, hands-on team where every agent works closely with the Principal Broker and has direct visibility into every file. You'll manage active listings and buyer relationships while relying on our in-house call center, interpretation, and bookkeeping teams to support the parts of the job that fall outside real estate itself.

Why You Should Join Us

  • Commission-based earning potential with competitive splits (details discussed during interview)
  • Warm client leads generated through our call center and marketing efforts
  • Administrative and bookkeeping support handled in-house, so you can focus on clients
  • Flexible, field-based schedule built around showings and client availability
  • Direct mentorship from the Principal Broker

What We Are Looking For

  • Client-focused professional who is organized and responsive
  • Comfortable managing multiple active files and deadlines at once
  • Must be 18+ years of age with a valid driver's license
  • High school diploma or GED equivalent

Key Skills

  • Active Florida real estate license in good standing (required)
  • Strong negotiation and communication skills
  • Working knowledge of purchase agreements, disclosures, and closing documents
  • English required; French, Haitian Creole, or Spanish a strong plus

Call Center Representative

Req ID#: LP-10232  ·  Location: Remote, US  ·  Reports to: Operations Lead

Job Description

Join our team as a Call Center Representative, where you'll serve as the first point of contact for our clients and their customers. You will play a vital role in creating a professional, welcoming experience on every call — scheduling appointments, answering questions, and routing issues to the right person. Fluency in more than one language is a strong asset, as our clients and their customers span English, French, Haitian Creole, and Spanish speakers.

Why You Should Join Us

  • Competitive hourly pay based on experience
  • The flexibility of a remote work environment
  • Paid training to get you fully up to speed before you start taking calls
  • Ongoing coaching and opportunities to grow into other roles as we expand
  • Supportive, small-team environment — you're never just a number

What We Are Looking For

  • Excellent listening and communication skills, both written and verbal
  • Ability to multitask and navigate between screens while assisting a customer
  • Comfortable working independently with minimal supervision
  • Must be 18+ years of age with a high school diploma or GED equivalent
  • Must pass a basic background check

Key Skills

  • Handle inbound calls and schedule appointments across multiple calendars
  • Bilingual in English/French, English/Creole, or English/Spanish strongly preferred
  • Comfortable using scheduling and CRM software
  • Accurate call logging and follow-up documentation

Home Setup Requirements

  • A hardwired or reliable high-speed internet connection
  • A quiet, distraction-free workspace
  • A computer or laptop capable of running scheduling and CRM software

About Luxtremous Property LLC

Luxtremous Property LLC is a multi-service company built around real estate — buying, selling, and renting property — along with the call center, interpretation, sales, and bookkeeping support that individuals and small businesses need around a transaction. We are a small, growing team that works with English, French, Haitian Creole, and Spanish-speaking clients, and every team member has direct visibility into the clients they serve rather than being lost in a large organization.

We're building a company where each hire matters, where clear communication comes first, and where there is real room to grow as we add clients and service lines.
